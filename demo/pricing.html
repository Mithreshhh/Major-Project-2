Common questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades start immediately and you pay only the difference. Downgrades start at your next billing date.

Is there a refund?

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days. Monthly plans are not refunded.

Do students get a discount?

Yes. Students get 50% off Pro with a valid college email address.

What happens to my files if I cancel?

They stay available for 30 days so you can download them, then they are deleted.
